Every publication starts with a reason to write. An idea you keep returning to. Something you've learned. A point of view you'd like to share with a few more people.

Then comes everything around the writing: choosing a platform, assembling a homepage, finding a layout that feels like you. A small idea can acquire a surprisingly long to-do list.

We're building **Lettercamp** to make that part simpler.

## A home for your writing

The idea is straightforward: a good-looking content site, thoughtful defaults, and room to make it your own. A place where a short update and a long essay both feel at home.

We want the writing to be the reason someone stays. Clear type, useful navigation, and a welcoming homepage should help readers find their way into it.

> Start with something worth sharing. Give it a place to live.

## Built around the post

A post can be a product announcement, a field guide, a personal essay, or a collection of things you've found. Sometimes it needs one image and a few paragraphs. Sometimes it needs sections, lists, and a quote that deserves a little breathing room.

[Take a look at an example](/posts/what-a-post-on-lettercamp-looks-like/) to see the kinds of stories we're designing for.

## Starting small

This blog is our starting point. We'll use it to share what we're making, explain the decisions behind Lettercamp, and learn by publishing here ourselves.

A few things we're aiming for:

- **A simple place to begin.** A homepage and a first post should be enough to get going.
- **A publication that feels like yours.** Your voice should lead the way.
- **Content you can take with you.** We're building around readable Markdown and MDX files.

We're still building. For now, welcome to the blog. This is where we'll leave our notes along the way.
