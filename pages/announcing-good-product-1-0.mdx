Today we're releasing **Good Product 1.0**, our shared workspace for the parts of teamwork that tend to fall between tools: the weekly update, the decision behind a change, and the handoff that makes the next step possible.

We opened the first version six weeks ago. It could publish a post, collect replies, and send a notification. Useful, but incomplete. You could tell people what happened. Finding the answer a week later was another matter.

Version 1.0 brings those pieces together.

## One place to catch up

A project has a home with its latest update, open decisions, and handoff notes. New teammates can follow the story without reading a month of chat history. People returning from a few days away can see what changed before asking for a recap.

Updates support rich text, checklists, links, and attachments. Drafts save as you write, including when your connection drops. Publishing still requires a connection; the editor makes that distinction explicit.

## Decisions that stay attached to the work

A decision records the question, the owner, the options, and the outcome. Once it is settled, the reasoning remains alongside the project instead of disappearing into a thread.

We introduced [Decisions earlier this month](/introducing-decisions/). In 1.0, you can also link a decision directly from an update, so readers can follow the context without searching for it.

## A quieter way to stay informed

Follow the projects you care about. Mentions and explicit requests appear in your inbox; routine updates can arrive in a weekly digest. Reading a project does not automatically subscribe you to every future conversation.

The [Friday digest](/meet-your-friday-digest/) is organized by project and links back to the original posts. It gives you a way to catch up without keeping another feed open all day.

## What 1.0 means to us

This is a deliberately focused release. Good Product does not have a chat room, a sprint planner, or an automated meeting recorder. It works alongside the tools a team already uses.

Our next work is less glamorous and just as important: better imports, clearer access controls, and faster search in larger workspaces. Today, we're happy to give the everyday work of keeping each other informed a proper home.
