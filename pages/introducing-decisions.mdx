You find the final design, but not the reason it changed. Someone remembers a customer constraint. Someone else thinks the original approach failed a test. The person who made the call is on leave.

Today we're introducing **Decisions** in Good Product: a small, structured place to record a question, work through the options, and leave an answer the next person can understand.

## Start with the question

Create a decision inside a project and give it an owner. Add the question you need to resolve, the options under consideration, and a target date if the work depends on an answer.

A decision starts in **Open**. Teammates can contribute context and discuss tradeoffs in the replies. The owner is responsible for moving it forward; the role does not imply that everyone else loses their voice.

## Close it with a reason

When the team is ready, the owner marks the decision **Decided** and writes the outcome. We ask for a short rationale because “Option B” is rarely enough context a month later.

For example:

> We'll launch with email invitations only. Domain-based access would make onboarding faster, but we need to finish the workspace permissions model first. Revisit after the access-control release.

That note records the choice, the tradeoff, and a useful condition for reopening it.

## Keep changes visible

A decided item can be reopened when new information arrives. Its earlier outcome remains in the history, so a changed decision does not look like an unexplained edit.

We deliberately left out approval chains and voting scores in this first version. A decision owner, a discussion, and a recorded outcome cover the process we want to support today. More formal sign-off would need a different model, not a checkbox bolted onto this one.

## Connected to the rest of the project

Open decisions appear beside the project's latest update. Decided items stay searchable, and you can link them from a handoff or a new post.

The feature is included in the current preview and will be part of [Good Product 1.0](/announcing-good-product-1-0/). The goal is simple: make the next “why did we do it this way?” a question your workspace can answer.
